> **In brief:**
> 1. The Hamon law allows cancellation at any time after 1 year of contract, at no cost.
> 2. Outside the Hamon law, the standard notice period is 2 months before annual renewal.
> 3. Driving without insurance exposes you to a fine of up to 3,750 euros.

## Two procedures depending on the contract's age

Cancelling car insurance follows two distinct regimes depending on whether the contract is more or less than one year old.

### The Hamon law: cancellation at any time

- Applicable as soon as the contract is more than one year old
- No cancellation fees or penalties
- The new insurer can handle the cancellation paperwork

## Standard cancellation at renewal

| Procedure | Deadline | Formalities |
|---|---|---|
| Hamon law (after 1 year) | At any time | Letter or process via the new insurer |
| Cancellation at renewal | 2 months before the anniversary date | Registered letter with acknowledgment of receipt |
| Cancellation for legitimate reason (vehicle sold) | Immediate | Supporting document required |

## The step you should never skip

Taking out the new contract before the old one ends avoids any coverage gap: driving without insurance is an offence punishable by a fine of up to 3,750 euros.

## What happens to the no-claims bonus when switching insurers

The [no-claims bonus coefficient](/en/blog/no-claims-bonus-how-calculated-lower-it/) follows the policyholder via the claims history statement provided by the previous insurer, regardless of the cancellation procedure used.
