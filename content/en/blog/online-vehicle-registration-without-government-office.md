> **In brief:**
> 1. Since 2017, government counters no longer process vehicle registration requests, everything is done online.
> 2. Two options exist: the free official registration agency website, or a provider authorized by the State.
> 3. The average processing time ranges from a few days to 3 weeks.

## Why registration is now exclusively online

The 2017 reform removed in-person registration counters in favor of a fully digital process, managed through the national vehicle registration system.

### The two possible paths

- The official registration agency website, free of charge aside from taxes
- A private provider authorized by the Ministry of the Interior, such as [Cartegrise.com](https://www.cartegrise.com/), which charges a service fee to support the file

## What an authorized provider changes

### An intermediary, not the official body

An authorized provider is not the registration agency itself: it is a professional accredited by the State, allowed to submit registration files on its behalf. The main benefit is support on complex files (inheritance, imported vehicle, duplicate) where a filing error significantly delays processing.

| Criteria | Official agency website | Authorized provider |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free (excluding vehicle taxes) | Service fee in addition to taxes |
| Support | None | File review, assistance |
| Delay in case of file error | Rejection and resubmission | Assisted correction |

## Steps of the process

1. Gather the mandatory documents (purchase invoice, transfer certificate, roadworthiness test)
2. Create an account on the official website or submit the file to the chosen provider
3. Receive the provisional certificate, valid for one month, while waiting for the final one
