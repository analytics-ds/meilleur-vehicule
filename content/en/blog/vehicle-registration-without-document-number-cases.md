> **In brief**
> 1. No document number does not mean the procedure is impossible: on the official agency website, **only the certificate date** is mandatory.
> 2. Registration certificate from before **2009**: that is normal, the number did not exist. Any procedure moves the vehicle into the current system, with a new number in the format **AB-123-CD**.
> 3. Certificate lost or stolen: you need a **duplicate**, which carries a new document number. The tax is **13.76 euros** for a vehicle registered since 2009 and **2.76 euros** for a vehicle with the old number.
> 4. At an approved provider such as cartegrise.com (approved since 2009, processing and provisional certificate advertised **within 12 to 24 hours** of receiving the complete file), the documents are checked before submission.

A 2006 Clio bought from a friend, in good condition, with its original registration certificate carefully kept in the glovebox. The ownership change is started online, and the form asks for a document number. There is none on the document, and the seller has never heard of it either.

This blockage is common, and almost always harmless. It covers three different situations, which call for different responses: a registration certificate too old to have a number, a lost certificate that needs replacing, or a number that has changed after a reissue. Here is what to do in each case.

## Which solution depending on your situation

| Situation | What replaces the number | Official route | With an approved provider |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Registration certificate from before 2009** (old format 123 AB 45) | The issue date of the certificate | Leave the field blank, enter the date | File entered and documents checked before submission |
| **Registration certificate lost or stolen** | A duplicate, with a new number | Duplicate request online, tax of 13.76 euros (2.76 euros with the old number) | Duplicate handled (54.90 euros in service fees at cartegrise.com, excluding tax) |
| **"Document number not found"** | The number of the most recently issued certificate | "Make another request" section on the official site | File checked before submission |

## Your registration certificate dates from before 2009

### Why the number does not exist

The document number is the certificate's issue reference: 11 characters, the first 4 of which give the year of issue. It has only existed since the current registration system was introduced, in 2009. An older registration certificate belongs to the old national file, with a registration number in the format 123 AB 45: it never had a document number.

So this is neither an error nor an incomplete document. [cartegrise.com](https://www.cartegrise.com) explains how to [find the document number](https://www.cartegrise.com/carte-grise-detail/numero-de-formule) on a recent certificate, and why it is missing on older ones.

### Where to read the issue date

Instead of the number, it is the certificate's issue date that serves as the identifier. It appears in box I of the registration certificate. According to an official France Titres-ANTS reply of 9 April 2025, the document number field is not mandatory on the official agency website: only the certificate date is, and for a certificate in the old format, the number field stays blank.

### The move to the current format

Any procedure carried out on a vehicle still registered under the old system moves it into the current system. The vehicle receives a new number in the format AB-123-CD, and a new registration certificate, which this time will carry a document number. The plates then have to be replaced to display the new number.

On the cost side, an ownership change follows the usual scale: regional tax, plus an 11 euro management fee and a 2.76 euro delivery fee. The detail is covered in our article on [how much a registration certificate costs in 2026](/en/blog/how-much-does-vehicle-registration-cost-2026/).

## Your registration certificate is lost or stolen

### The duplicate

Without the document, the document number can no longer be read. The solution is to request a duplicate, meaning a reissue of the certificate. The process is done online on the official agency website or through an approved provider. In the event of theft, a report to the police comes before the request.

### A new number

The duplicate carries a new document number, which begins with the year of its own issue. The old number stops being valid: it is the duplicate's number that will need to be entered afterwards, for example on a transfer certificate.

### How much it costs

- **On the official route**: 13.76 euros in tax for a vehicle registered since 2009, and 2.76 euros for a vehicle with the old number. No regional tax is added, since ownership does not change.
- **At cartegrise.com**: 54.90 euros in service fees, on top of these taxes. The site lets you [request a duplicate registration certificate](https://www.cartegrise.com/demarches-carte-grise/duplicata) online, with a provisional certificate advertised within 12 to 24 hours of receiving the complete file.

## The site displays "document number not found"

### A number changed after a reissue

This message mainly surprises those who copy the number from an old document. An official France Titres-ANTS reply of 9 September 2025 confirms it: a document number can change after a reissue of the certificate. A duplicate requested some years ago, or a change that led to a new certificate, is enough to make the old number invalid.

First step: check that the number entered is indeed that of the most recent certificate, and that its first 4 digits match the year of the date written in box I. The two must match.

### The remedy provided by the official agency

If the entry is correct and the site still blocks it, the official agency provides a remedy through the "Make another request" section. The file is then handled outside the automatic process, within a timeframe that depends on the service's workload.

For a second-hand purchase, the seller also has to enter the correct number on the transfer certificate: an error here blocks the buyer's procedure. ## Going through an approved site: when is it useful?

The official route is enough in most cases, for free, as soon as the certificate date is known. A provider approved by the Ministry of the Interior becomes useful in three situations.

- **The certificate is lost and time is short.** It handles the duplicate and issues a provisional certificate, which lets you drive for 1 month in France.
- **The file has already been refused.** It checks the documents before submission, which avoids a second round trip.
- **The ownership change needs to go through quickly.** At cartegrise.com, fees range from 44.90 to 99.90 euros depending on the regional tax amount, with processing advertised within 12 to 24 hours of receiving the complete file.

In every case, cartegrise.com remains an approved provider, separate from the official agency.
