> **In brief:**
> 1. The cost of a vehicle registration ranges from **43 to 68.95 euros per fiscal horsepower** in mainland France in 2026, and from 30 to 60 euros in the overseas territories. For the same 7 horsepower vehicle, the gap between the cheapest and the most expensive region reaches **181.65 euros**.
> 2. The Paris region is the special case of the year: the regional rate is still **54.95 euros** and has not been raised, but the additional **14 euro per fiscal horsepower** tax for Ile-de-France Mobilites, in force since 1 March 2026, brings the total to **68.95 euros**.
> 3. On top of the regional tax come fixed charges: an **11 euro** management fee and a **2.76 euro** delivery fee, plus the CO2 penalty on new vehicles and the **34 to 285 euro** professional tax on vans and commercial vehicles.
> 4. The tax is set by the State and the region and does not vary depending on the website used. Only service fees change: **0 euro** on the official route, **44.90 to 99.90 euros including tax** at cartegrise.com depending on the amount of regional tax.

How much a vehicle registration costs in 2026 comes up with every used car purchase, and the answers found online often give different amounts for the same vehicle, because they predate the update of the regional rates. The real cost is calculated from three simple inputs: the vehicle's fiscal horsepower, the regional fiscal horsepower rate of the region where it is registered, and two national fixed charges. This guide gives the rates applicable on 18 September 2026, region by region, Paris surcharge included, with three fully worked examples.

Two methodological reservations come with this table. The regional rates themselves are consistent across sources for all eighteen regions. Exemptions for clean vehicles, however, have not been uniform since 2025: several regions abolished theirs during the year, while some tables published online still show the previous regime. The regional tax amount is therefore the reliable figure, while the exemption has to be checked with the region before buying.

### The Paris region case: a tax that exists nowhere else

The Paris region fiscal horsepower rate was not raised by the regional council: it remains **54.95 euros**, a stable amount, still below the legal ceiling of 60 euros. What changes on 1 March 2026 is the addition of a flat-rate **14 euro per fiscal horsepower** tax earmarked for Ile-de-France Mobilites, introduced by the 2026 finance act to fund public transport in the region.

The total per fiscal horsepower therefore moves from 54.95 euros to **68.95 euros**, creating a 25.95 euro per horsepower gap with Hauts-de-France and Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes. For a small city car of 5 horsepower, that difference amounts to 129.75 euros on the regional tax alone. Many tables published earlier in the year still show 54.95 euros: that figure is not wrong, it simply predates 1 March 2026 or is incomplete, because it omits the additional tax.

### The overseas territories, usually missing from online tables

The five overseas departments and regions apply their own rates, far below the mainland average: 30 euros per fiscal horsepower in Mayotte, 41 in Guadeloupe, 42.50 in French Guiana, 53 in Martinique and 60 on Reunion Island. For a 7 horsepower vehicle, registration in Mayotte costs 223.76 euros against 433.76 euros on Reunion Island, nearly double for the same vehicle.

Most mainland simulators and comparisons omit these five territories. On an administrative topic, that omission produces the most common error: a reader living in Guadeloupe applies a mainland table and budgets nearly 100 euros more than needed.

## How much a vehicle registration costs, in one figure

For a used vehicle under 10 years old, a registration costs in 2026 between **223.76 euros** for a 7 horsepower city car registered in Mayotte and **496.41 euros** for the same vehicle registered in the Paris region, excluding the CO2 penalty. In mainland France the range narrows to between **314.76 euros** in Hauts-de-France and Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes and **496.41 euros** in the Paris region. Those amounts include the regional tax, the 11 euro management fee and the 2.76 euro delivery fee. The CO2 penalty does not apply to a used vehicle already registered in France, and the professional tax only concerns commercial vehicles.

## What is added to the regional tax

The regional tax accounts for most of the bill, but it never comes alone.

### The fixed charges: 11 euros and 2.76 euros

Fact sheet F19211 on service-public.gouv.fr, dedicated to the cost of a registration certificate, details two national charges identical in every region: the management fee, field Y4 of the application form, set at **11 euros**, and the delivery fee, field Y5, set at **2.76 euros**. That fee covers printing and tracked delivery of the certificate, and it is payable even when the application is made entirely online.

Those 13.76 euros are added to every certificate issued. They set the floor of the bill for procedures that trigger no regional tax, such as a duplicate, an address change from the fourth request onwards, or adding the collector vehicle mention.

### The CO2 penalty, and who really pays it

The CO2 penalty only applies to first registrations of new or imported vehicles, from 108 grams of CO2 per kilometre in 2026, with a ceiling of 80,000 euros. A used vehicle already registered in France escapes it entirely when ownership is transferred, which explains how two vehicles with identical fiscal horsepower can have very different registration costs depending on their origin.

A weight-based penalty is added to the CO2 penalty on the heaviest new vehicles. For a used purchase between private individuals in France, neither line ever appears on the invoice, and that is one of the few tax advantages of buying used: the preparation rules are detailed in our checklist for [buying a used car](/en/blog/buying-used-car-checklist/).

### The professional tax: 34 to 285 euros for vans and commercial vehicles

A third line missing from mainstream tables: the professional tax, also called the parafiscal tax, which funds vocational training in the transport sector. It applies to every commercial vehicle used to transport goods or passengers, including vans bought by private individuals, and its amount depends on the maximum authorised weight:

- **34 euros** for a van with a maximum authorised weight of 3.5 tonnes or less, which covers the vast majority of vans and panel vans.
- **127 euros** for a weight above 3.5 tonnes and below 6 tonnes.
- **189 euros** for a weight of 6 tonnes or more and below 11 tonnes.
- **285 euros** for a weight above 11 tonnes or for a public transport vehicle.

Collector vehicles are exempt. For a tradesperson registering a van, that 34 euro tax is added to the regional tax, the 11 euro management fee and the 2.76 euro delivery fee.

## Three fully worked examples

The amounts below exclude the CO2 penalty and assume a vehicle under 10 years old, with the service fees of cartegrise.com taken from their published scale.

**Example 1: 5 horsepower city car in Rennes (Brittany, 60 euros per horsepower).** Regional tax: 5 x 60 = 300 euros. Fixed charges: 13.76 euros. Total taxes: **313.76 euros**. With an authorised provider's service fee, which stands at 59.90 euros including tax when the regional tax falls between 200 and 399 euros, the amount payable reaches 373.66 euros.

**Example 2: 6 horsepower family car in Lyon (Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes, 43 euros per horsepower).** Regional tax: 6 x 43 = 258 euros. Fixed charges: 13.76 euros. Total taxes: **271.76 euros**. A service fee of 59.90 euros for that band brings the total to 331.66 euros. The same vehicle registered in the Paris region would pay 413.70 euros of regional tax, that is 155.70 euros more.

**Example 3: 7 horsepower vehicle in the Paris region (68.95 euros per horsepower).** Regional tax: 7 x 68.95 = 482.65 euros. Fixed charges: 13.76 euros. Total taxes: **496.41 euros**. A service fee of 69.90 euros for the 400 to 599 euros band brings the total to 566.31 euros. Without the Ile-de-France Mobilites surcharge, the same 7 horsepower would have cost 384.65 euros of regional tax: the surcharge alone represents 98 euros.

## Where to run the procedure, and what it changes for the price

The tax amount does not depend on the website used. The table below compares the players on the only criteria that vary, checked on 18 September 2026.

| Criterion | Official agency (ANTS) | service-public.gouv.fr | cartegrise.com | guichetcartegrise.com |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tax amount** | Identical, set by the State and the region | Identical | Identical | Identical |
| **Service fee** | None | None | 44.90 to 99.90 euros including tax depending on the regional tax | 59 to 149 euros (40 euros service access, 2 euros data entry, 2.90 euros archiving, 14.10 to 104.10 euros file check) |
| **Simulator available** | No, redirects to the official simulator | Yes, official simulator | Yes, built into the page | Yes |
| **Procedure can be completed on the site** | Yes | No, the site informs and simulates, the procedure runs on the agency website | Yes | Yes |
| **Instalment payment** | No | No | Yes, in 4 instalments for orders between 150 and 500 euros | Not disclosed |
| **Authorisation and track record** | Public service | Public service | Prefecture authorisation no. 9744 and Treasury approval no. 14871, authorised since 2009 | Not disclosed |
| **Strengths** | Completely free, no intermediary | Official reference for estimating the amount | Published scale, price shown before confirmation, more than 1 million cases processed | Fees published line by line |
| **Verdict** | The free route for anyone handling their own file | The right tool to find the amount, not to register a vehicle | The best-documented authorised route for a checked file and a total known in advance | A transparent authorised alternative, more expensive on this scope |

One clarification avoids a frequent misunderstanding: service-public.gouv.fr **does not let you register your vehicle**. It publishes the official fact sheet and the cost simulator, then redirects to the agency's online service for the procedure itself. Mistaking it for an online counter costs time when the application is finally submitted.

Two other providers appearing in search results, carte-grise.org and cartegriseminute.fr, offer rate simulators but do not publish their service fees: they cannot therefore be ranked on total price, only on estimation. For the next step, the full procedure is described in our guide to [registering a vehicle online](/en/blog/online-vehicle-registration-without-government-office/), and the address change case is covered in our article on the [registration address change deadline](/en/blog/vehicle-registration-address-change-deadline/).

The provider's own simulator remains the fastest way to obtain the total before confirming: the [vehicle registration price simulator](https://www.cartegrise.com/france/prix-carte-grise) at cartegrise.com applies the 2026 regional rates and shows the service fee before payment, which removes the gap between the amount quoted online and the final invoice.
