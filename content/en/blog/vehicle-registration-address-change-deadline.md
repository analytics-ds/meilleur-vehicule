> **In brief:**
> 1. The legal deadline to update the address is one month after moving.
> 2. A flat-rate fine of 135 euros sanctions failure to update in case of a check.
> 3. The process is free aside from a delivery fee of around 2.76 euros.

## A legal obligation often overlooked

Updating the address on the vehicle registration is a legal obligation under the traffic code, too often neglected after moving.

### The deadline to respect

- 1 month from the moving date to complete the process
- 135 euros flat-rate fine in case of a check with an outdated address
- No filing fee, only the document delivery fee remains due

## The process step by step

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Proof of address | Less than 6 months old, showing the new address |
| Online filing | On the official agency website or via an authorized provider |
| Receipt | New certificate sent within a few days to 3 weeks |

### Why use an authorized provider

For a simple file, the free process on the official agency website is enough. For a more complex file (several undeclared moves, missing document), a provider authorized by the State such as [Cartegrise.com](https://www.cartegrise.com/) can review the file before submission and avoid a rejection that would extend the processing time.

## What does not change

The registration number stays identical for life, regardless of the number of moves or ownership changes: only the address on the certificate is updated.
